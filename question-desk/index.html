Evidence Question Desk

Ask a question about metformin. The desk drafts an answer from live public sources, then asks the Metformin Evidence Steward to check it for lost context, so qualifiers, superseded facts and label status survive the trip to you.

Ask the desk

Tell the desk your market (US, UK or Germany) and ask about metformin: approved uses, warnings, or the evidence on a use such as long COVID, cancer or ageing.

Your messages and the sources' data go to the LLM provider you choose in the settings below. Before the desk contacts the steward, your browser shows a card with what it will send.

How an answer is made

  1. Draft. The desk reads live sources: the US label (openFDA), the trial registry (ClinicalTrials.gov) and PubMed, and drafts a short answer.
  2. Consent. Your browser asks whether to contact the Metformin Evidence Steward, and shows the start of the request.
  3. Check. The steward's agents run in your browser and check the draft in code: dropped qualifiers, superseded claims, label status for your market.
  4. Answer with a receipt. You get the corrected answer, what changed, the live sources and the claim ids used.
  5. Fail closed. If the steward cannot check the draft, or you decline, the desk does not give the draft as an answer. You get only the sources.
